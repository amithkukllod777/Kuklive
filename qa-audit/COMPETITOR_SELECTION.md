# Competitor Selection — Kuklive

**Date checked:** 2026-07-14 · **Platform focus:** Android / Android TV · **Region:** India-first, global playlists.

## Product framing
- **Category:** IPTV / M3U live-TV player (bring-your-own-playlist), not a licensed content service.
- **Target users:** viewers who load free public playlists or their provider's M3U/Xtream account; cord-cutters, Android TV / FireTV owners.
- **Business model:** free, no accounts. **Not** a paid content provider (unlike Tata Play/JioTV/Airtel — those are licensed OTT services and are *not* direct competitors; they are a different segment and are only referenced for category conventions).
- **Platforms:** Android phone/TV + LG webOS.

## Evidence rules applied
No live web/store verification was performed in this session. All competitor capabilities below are **INFERRED** from widely-known product behaviour and should be **re-verified** on the Play Store / official docs before acting. Do not treat as VERIFIED.

|---|---|---|---|
| **TiViMate** | Market leader (Android TV) | The reference M3U/Xtream player for Android TV; sets table-stakes (EPG, multi-playlist, recording in premium). | INFERRED (well-known) |

**Close substitutes / context (not scored as primary):** GSE Smart IPTV, Perfect Player, Free-TV-style simple apps, and the licensed OTT apps (JioTV, Tata Play, Airtel Xstream) which define the *category naming* users expect but serve a different (licensed, paid) market.

## Limitations
- Feature availability, pricing, and platform support change frequently — **verify per store listing and region**.
- Some competitor features are gated behind one-time/premium purchases; free-tier parity differs from premium parity.
- Kuklive is free/no-account; comparing its free experience to competitors' premium tiers would be misleading and is avoided.
